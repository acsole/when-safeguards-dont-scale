# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level, including people who, like the maintainer, are relatively new to AI safety, to open source, or to writing and publishing this kind of work. The goal is a genuinely safe, low-friction place to learn, ask questions, and contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a technical or analytical point.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion in issues and pull requests comes from missing context, not bad intent.
- Welcome beginner questions. If something in the documents is unclear, that is a documentation bug, not a user error: please open an issue.
- Give credit where it is due, and cite sources accurately (including AI-assisted contributions, see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- Respect differing viewpoints on open questions. How safeguards behave at scale, and how oversight should work, are areas of active debate, not settled matters.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, or any associated community channel).
- Deliberately submitting malicious content, prompt-injection payloads, or anything designed to exfiltrate secrets or harm other contributors' systems.
- Sharing anyone's private information without explicit consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberate derailing of issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately by opening a confidential report through GitHub's private reporting mechanism, or by contacting the maintainer directly at the email listed on the repository profile (`acsole`). Reports will be reviewed and responded to as promptly as possible. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
